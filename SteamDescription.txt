Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) translation for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3680501610]World Domination 2.0[/url] by TSA.

為 TSA 的 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3680501610]World Domination 2.0[/url] 提供繁體中文翻譯。

[h2]What is translated[/h2][list]
[*]Mod settings and the WD tab (diplomacy, outposts, world stats, action log)
[*]Outpost types, outpost upgrades, roads, road blocks and traps
[*]Raids, spy operations, mortar outposts, travelers, alerts, letters and quests
[/list]
Terms follow the vanilla Traditional Chinese translation. Translated from the English text; the bundled Simplified Chinese was used only as a reference.

[h2]翻譯範圍[/h2][list]
[*]模組設定與 WD 分頁（外交、哨站、世界統計、行動紀錄）
[*]哨站類型、哨站升級、道路、路障與陷阱
[*]突襲、間諜行動、砲兵哨站、旅行者、警示、信件與任務
[/list]
用詞依照原版遊戲的繁體中文翻譯，以英文原文為準翻譯，內附的簡體中文僅作參考。

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Load after World Domination 2.0.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]Release notes are left in English.
[*]World Domination updates often. Text added after this translation shows in English until the translation is updated.
[/list]
I made this because I wanted to play World Domination and no Traditional Chinese translation existed yet. If anyone would like to take over the translation, you are welcome to.
